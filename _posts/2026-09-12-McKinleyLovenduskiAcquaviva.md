The McKinley group continued our regular tradition of getting together with the group of [Nikki Lovenduski from University of Colorado Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu/instaar/research/labs-groups/ocean-biogeochemistry-research-group) for two days of discussion on ocean carbon, biogeochemistry and climate using data, models and machine learning. We were lucky that some of the group of [Viviana Acquaviva of CUNY-City Tech Physics](https://vacquaviva.github.io/terrad2i/) could join us. Can't wait to do it again soon!
